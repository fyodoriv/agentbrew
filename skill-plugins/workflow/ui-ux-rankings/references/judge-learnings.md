# Judge learnings

[design-judges.md](design-judges.md) or `scripts/judge.mjs`, with a test.

|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-08 | sidequest Gifts | Free 0-10 scores from 10 blind judges: 5.0, 6.0, 6.0, 6.2, 6.25 over five rounds, through a structural redesign; 0 judges at 8 | Free scores stall near 6 and every judge names exactly the asked-for 5 issues. Score by issues with a rubric, ask for "at most 8, or NONE", and pass settled decisions |

| 2026-11-08 | sidequest Gifts | Judges flagged an icon-only button as "unlabeled" though it had an aria-label and a title | Screenshots hide labels and tooltips: say in `context.txt` which icon buttons carry a label, or show a visible word |
| 2026-11-08 | sidequest Gifts | Issue-scored panel, round 1: mean 8.15, 9/10 at 8+; the product lens alone gave 5.8 (3 majors: no visible outcome of Keep, no in-time delivery cue, taste contradicting history) | The product lens finds goal-level gaps the visual lenses miss; fix its majors first. Check each new layout on the phone shots yourself: a nowrap fix pushed the subtitle off screen |
| 2026-11-08 | sidequest Gifts | Round 3 after fixing the product majors (reason per row, in-time delivery, no avoids that contradict loved gifts): mean 8.44, min 7.8, 8/10 at 8+; next consensus is the phone card pushing Keep below the first screen | Fixing the one lens with majors moved the mean most. 2 of 10 judges returned their reply but did not write the file: save each reply from its result before you score |
| 2026-11-09 | sidequest, 16 views | 5-lens rounds (product, hierarchy, interaction, mobile, a11y) took 10 views to 8+ on the first pass; three views stalled near 7.5 for two rounds, then passed after a layout change: filters folded into one summary row (Activities 7.5 → 8.24), a dead-end card given a button (People 7.66 → 8.04), a wrapped caption put on its own line (Restaurants 7.1 → 8.9) | Fix only issues that 2+ judges name. When two rounds gain under 0.3, change the layout, not the polish. Re-run the full gate after the round: a UI test pinned old chip markup twice |
| 2026-11-09 | cashcast, 20 views | One agent asked to judge all 10 lenses over 40 shots ran 4h23m and wrote no file; nobody noticed because no watchdog was armed | Run at most five lenses per judge agent, tell it to write `r/<lens>.txt` right after each lens, time-box it (~20 min), and arm a background watchdog on `r/` with a 30 min cap. Before shots, check `dist` and the fixture are fresh and restart the fixture server |
